# Creator

A profile for people whose work lives on other platforms. It leads with the one thing they want a visitor to do,
shows their latest work as a carousel, and gathers every platform in one place, with a separate door for brands.

Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) first: it holds the rules for every recipe. This guide adds what is specific here.

## Who this is for

Video creators, streamers, influencers and podcasters. People who mainly want to show written or reported work
should start from [journalist](../journalist/GUIDE.md).

## The layout

| # | Card | Kind | Width | Surface | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Main action | buttons | full | filled | Subscribe, newsletter or support: one clear ask |
| 2 | Latest videos | collection, carousel | full | plain | Three to six recent pieces with thumbnails |
| 3 | Where I post | links, grid | full | filled | Every platform, icons derived from the URLs |
| 4 | Brand partnerships | buttons | half | outlined | Optional: how brands reach them |
| 5 | About | readme | half | filled | Their story and house rules |

If card 4 is dropped, set card 5's `width` to `full`.

## Interview

1. `displayName`, `headline`: their name and what they make, with their schedule if they have one.
2. `mainAction`: the one thing a visitor should do. One main button; a second only if it is clearly secondary.
3. `latest`: three to six recent pieces. They upload each thumbnail in the editor.
4. `platforms`: profile links for every platform they post on.
5. `partnerships`: whether brands may contact them, and which address may be public.
6. `about`, `location`, `website`, `avatar`.

## Writing rules

- One main action. A page that asks for five things gets none of them.
- Titles are copied exactly as published.
- Platform links are the account URL only; the app shows each platform's icon and the account name.
- Keep the sponsorship disclosure line, in their words. Many countries require creators to disclose paid content.
- No follower counts or view numbers: they go stale and the page cannot update them.

## Before publishing

- [ ] The first button goes where they most want visitors to go.
- [ ] Every platform link opens their own account.
- [ ] The person confirmed each published email address may be public.
- [ ] Thumbnails are their own images.
